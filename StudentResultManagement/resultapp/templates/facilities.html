Sports

The university encourages students to participate in indoor and outdoor sports activities. Facilities for cricket, football, badminton, volleyball, and athletics are available. Sports events and competitions are organized regularly to improve teamwork, fitness, leadership qualities, and overall personality development among students.

NCC / NSS

NCC and NSS programs help students develop discipline, leadership, and social responsibility. Students participate in social service activities, awareness campaigns, camps, and community development programs. These activities encourage patriotism, confidence, teamwork, and responsibility toward society and the nation.

Hostel Facilities

The university provides comfortable hostel facilities for boys and girls with proper safety, cleanliness, and security. Hostel rooms are well-maintained and include Wi-Fi, study areas, dining facilities, and recreational spaces to ensure a safe and supportive environment for students.

Placement Cell and Activities

The placement cell organizes training sessions, workshops, mock interviews, and campus recruitment drives. Students receive guidance in communication skills, aptitude preparation, resume building, and career planning to improve job opportunities and professional development.

Health Facilities

Health facilities are available for students and staff to ensure medical support during emergencies. The university provides first-aid services, regular health check-ups, medical consultation, and awareness programs related to physical and mental well-being.

Anti-Ragging Cell

The Anti-Ragging Cell ensures a safe and friendly campus environment for all students. Strict rules are followed to prevent ragging and harassment. Awareness programs and monitoring activities are conducted regularly to maintain discipline and student safety within the campus.

Opportunity Cell

The Opportunity Cell guides students regarding internships, scholarships, higher education, skill development, and career opportunities. The cell organizes seminars, counseling sessions, and career awareness programs to help students achieve academic and professional success.

Labs

The university provides modern laboratories equipped with advanced tools and technologies. Students gain practical knowledge through experiments, research activities, and hands-on learning. Labs are maintained properly to support innovation, creativity, and technical skill development.
