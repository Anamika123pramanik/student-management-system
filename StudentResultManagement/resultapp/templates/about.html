ABOUT SHAMIKA UNIVERSITY


SHAMIKA University is one of the leading Universities in the East India Region and the first private University in the entire Kolhan Region (3 districts of East Singhbhum, West Singhbhum and Saraikela-Kharsawan).

The University was established by the JHARKHAND State Legislature under “The SHAMIKA University Act” in the year 2017. SAU is recognized by UGC.

SHAMIKA University is promoted & managed by SHAMIKA Educational & Cultural Trust (sponsoring body), a trust incorporated under trust act, registered under Registrar of Government of Karnataka, Registration No: BSG – 4 – 00138 – 2009 dated 21st November 2009.

An illustrious 15-year legacy characterizes the success of the University in terms of carving a niche for itself through a gamut of diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes (prior to becoming a private university, SHAMIKA College provided quality higher education since 2011).

The university has affiliations, recognitions and memberships from such statutory and affiliating bodies as AICTE, BCI, PCI, INC, JNRC, AIU, ASCO India.

Recognized in the R World Institutional Ranking – Green Rankings 2026, achieving the prestigious Titanium+ Band for its accelerated impact in sustainable education.

SAU is ranked 38th Best Private University in the Country in the latest THE WEEK Hansa Research Best Universities Survey 2025.
SAU is ranked 52nd Best Private University in the Country in the India Today MDRA Best Universities Survey 2024.

7000-plus students, 40 programmes (at diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral levels), 7 schools, Research & Development Cell, and 190-plus faculty members are the hallmarks of the University.

Programmes are offered in the domains of engineering, technology, management, commerce, pharmacy, English, mass communication, fashion design, law, nursing, allied health science, research.

Vision

The vision of the Arts Department is to develop enlightened, knowledgeable, and responsible individuals who can contribute positively to society. The department aims to create an academic environment where students are encouraged to think independently, explore new ideas, and understand the world from different perspectives. It focuses on nurturing intellectual curiosity, creativity, and ethical values among students. The ultimate vision is to prepare students who are not only academically strong but also socially aware and capable of making meaningful contributions in various fields of life.

Mission

The mission of the Arts Department is to provide quality education in humanities and social science subjects. It focuses on improving students’ communication skills, analytical thinking, and creativity. The department also encourages students to understand real-world issues and develop solutions through knowledge and awareness.
