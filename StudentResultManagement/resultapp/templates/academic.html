Library

Shamika University provides access to the information resources required to support the rigorous academic and professional demands of a top-tier university environment.
In addition to maintaining a comprehensive print collection, the library offers extensive access to a wide range of digital and online information resources. The experienced team of library professionals and academic support staff provides research guidance, personalized consultations, and email-based reference assistance, ensuring that students and faculty can effectively utilize all available resources. The library at Shamika University functions as an integrated Knowledge Resource Centre, equipped with a vast collection of books, magazines, Indian and international journals, as well as online databases and e-journals. These resources collectively support all areas of teaching, learning, research, and innovation across disciplines.
